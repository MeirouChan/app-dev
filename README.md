# The Seven Deadly Sins (Nanatsu no Taizai)

**The Seven Deadly Sins** is a *Japanese fantasy adventure* anime/manga series created by **Nakaba Suzuki**.
It follows a group of legendary knights who are framed for treason and must clear their names.

## Why I Like It

> "Who decided that?" — **Escanor**

Reasons:

- **Fun ensemble cast** — each Sin has a distinct personality
- *Big fantasy battles* and creative powers
- ~~Slow start~~ It gets more exciting as the story grows
- Strong themes of **loyalty and redemption**
- Escanor is absolutely iconic

### The Seven Sins

1. **Meliodas** — Dragon’s Sin of Wrath
2. **Ban** — Fox’s Sin of Greed
3. **King** — Grizzly’s Sin of Sloth
4. **Diane** — Serpent’s Sin of Envy
5. **Merlin** — Boar’s Sin of Gluttony
6. **Gowther** — Goat’s Sin of Lust
7. **Escanor** — Lion’s Sin of Pride
   - My favorite character
   - Famous for his confidence and power
